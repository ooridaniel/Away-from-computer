## Profile

I design and improve customer journeys in regulated credit products for people mainstream finance underserves. At Babban Gona I design the full lending journey, from eligibility and application through approval and repayment, for first-time borrowers on cheap Android phones and poor connections. That is credit inclusion as a design constraint rather than a slogan. I read sessions and product numbers together, find where customers drop out, and fix it: at Cavista, rebuilding journeys against search and behavioural data lifted engagement 40% and conversions 25% in three months, and a booking product I shipped reached 4.8 stars and 20% higher bookings by iterating on friction found in recorded sessions. Testing with more than 50 users lifted adoption 30%. I prototype quickly in Figma, work closely with developers (I build in React and WordPress myself), and champion accessibility in every journey.
