## Profile

I design for people with minutes, not hours: clinical and administrative staff between patients, and first-time users on poor connections. Three years in healthcare software taught me to design inside a regulated review cycle, where wording, claims and consent screens are checked before release, and to defend design decisions to clinical, legal and commercial reviewers with evidence rather than taste. At Babban Gona I run discovery and delivery side by side as the product's designer, in a small team without a large design function: user conversations, workflow observation, scoping, MVP, high-fidelity prototype, then measurement of what shipped. Testing with more than 50 hard-to-reach users lifted adoption 30%. I build and document the component library the product runs on, and I design AI behaviour (confidence, source citation, handing the decision back) so an automated answer never goes unchallenged. I am comfortable setting up design practice where little exists, and I use AI tools daily to speed up prototyping and research synthesis.
